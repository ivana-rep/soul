God, all my hope is in You. You are all i need.
thank You for being my protector and the source of my strength. in every situation, i ask for Your courage and endurance to face it and handle it well.
please guide me with Your wisdom as i make decisions, and fill me with peace as i do what You've called me to do. in Jesus' name, amen.
