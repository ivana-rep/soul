worry & anxiety | You are enough for me
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

Father, You know what i need and what i want.
and no matter what my current situation is, i believe You are enough for me.
Your providence is enough for me.
Your love is enough for me.
Your will is enough for me.
so today i let go of my worries for the future and my worries for the present.
i will seek You and trust that You will take care of me.
in Jesus' name, amen.
