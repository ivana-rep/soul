strength & trust | the One i can count on
↳ [back to index](index.html)

God, when my circumstances are challenging, i know that You are good.
when it seems like everything is going wrong, i am certain that You are making everything work together for good.
i have faith in Your plans and in Your love for me.
thank You for being the One i can count on.
in Jesus' name, amen.
