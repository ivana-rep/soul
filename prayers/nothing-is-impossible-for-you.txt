God, i am so grateful that nothing is impossible for You.
thank You for proving that over and over again.
and thank You for drawing near to me through prayer.
right now, please remind me of Your will for me.
speak to me in a fresh way, and help me to process all that You are revealing to me.
in Jesus' name, amen.
