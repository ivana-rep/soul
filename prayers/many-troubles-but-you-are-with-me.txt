strength & trust | many troubles, but You are with me
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, i know that in this world i will have many troubles, but i also know that You are with me.
i'm grateful i can trust You to guide and protect me, no matter what i face.
please give me peace during times of trials and challenges, and allow me to rest in the promises You've made.
in Jesus' name, amen.
