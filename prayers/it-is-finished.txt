love & grace | it is finished
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

Jesus,
it pains me that the torture and pain You endured on the cross were necessary to save humanity.
You didn't want us to be slaves to fear, anxiety, or sin, so You sacrificed Yourself so that Love could win.
it is Love that hung on the cross and gave up everything to save us.
"it is finished" was a cry of victory, because You defeated everything that held us in slavery.
because of Your sacrifice, i can experience intimacy with You forever. thank You!
show me how to share Your love today and every day.
use me to reach the world for whose redemption You died.
amen.
