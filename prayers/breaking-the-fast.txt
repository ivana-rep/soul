Father, thank You for carrying me through these days.
as i eat again, let me do it with gratitude, not in a hurry.
whatever You started in me during this fast, don't let it end with it.
!!keep the hunger for You that remains when the hunger for food is gone!!.
i receive what You've given me, and i trust You with what i haven't seen yet.
amen.
