God, i stop for a moment in the middle of this day, and i turn back to You.
You have carried me this far, even when i forgot You were there.
please give me patience for what is left, and kindness for the people i meet.
wherever my loved ones are right now, keep them healthy, keep them happy, keep them close to You.
!!bring my thoughts back to You!!, whenever they wander toward what does not come from You.
in Jesus' name, amen.
