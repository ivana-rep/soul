God, thank You for giving me strength in every situation.
despite my best efforts, i cannot do anything on my own.
sometimes i let stress get the better of me and forget that You are in control.
fill me with Your strength and guide me through this time, one day at a time.
in Jesus' name, amen.

---

come back to this prayer when you feel like it's too heavy the burden on your shoulders, and you feel helpless and alone.
you are not, for God is with you, so pray for strength and the ability to let go, and let Him guide you.
