God, it's hard to keep planting seeds when i'm discouraged.
when i'm heartbroken.
but i know that You can use all things for Your glory and my good.
once again, i'm trusting You.
i'm surrendering my plans to You — because Your ways are always better.
thank You in advance for the harvest that's to come.
in Jesus' name, amen.
