obedience | not my will, but Yours
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=prayers/non-la-mia-volonta-ma-la-tua.txt)

Lord, i want to be completely yielded to You.
show me any part of my heart i've held back from Your authority — any desire i've refused to surrender to Your will.
give me the grace to die to myself daily.
!!not my will, but Yours be done!!.
in Jesus' name, amen.

---

surrender isn't a single decision, it's a daily one.
the areas we're most reluctant to name before God are usually the exact areas we haven't yielded.
but He already knows what's there — saying it out loud isn't for His benefit, it's for yours.
the Holy Spirit is already in you. let Him work. stop getting in His way.
dying to self isn't dramatic — it's small and repeated, one choice at a time.
