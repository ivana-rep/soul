prayer & devotion | help me forgive the way You forgive
↳ [back to index](index.html)

father, thank You for inviting me into Your power.
for the simplicity of prayer.
and for Your mercy.
Lord, when i pray, help my unbelief.
when i pray, push me further.
when i pray, help me to forgive the way You forgive — completely, totally, selflessly.
amen.
