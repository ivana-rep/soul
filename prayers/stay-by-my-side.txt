grief & comfort | stay by my side
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

Lord, when my heart is in pieces, help me trust You.
I don't need You to explain everything — I just need You to stay by my side.
remind me that faith was never the promise of a life without suffering,
but the promise of a God who enters my pain and doesn't leave me alone.
even when I can't see the meaning today, You haven't stopped writing my story.
in Jesus' name, amen.

---

trusting God when everything goes well is easy. trusting Him when your heart is in pieces — that's faith.
He doesn't always explain the reason. He doesn't cancel the pain.
but He stays.
faith was never the promise of a life without suffering — it's the promise of a God who enters your suffering and doesn't leave you alone.
if Jesus chose to share our pain on the cross, then your pain is not ignored.
in His hands, pain is never empty.

---
