God, in the middle of this day of rest, i stop for a moment and turn back to You.
You have carried me through this week, even when i forgot You were there.
these days at home can easily unsettle my mind: old tensions, sharp words, expectations i cannot meet.
and when i react badly, it can make me feel guilty and small.
please reframe what i feel here: my worth does not come from pleasing my family, it comes from You.
give me the courage to say no with kindness, and the peace to not explain myself more than needed.
help me love the people around me without carrying what is not mine to carry.
when stress pushes me toward food, remind me that it cannot fill what only You can fill. let me eat with gratitude, not to escape.
give me patience for what is left of this day.
bless my family: those under this roof and those far away. keep them healthy, keep them happy, keep them close to You. let me be kind to each of them, and kind to myself.
thank You for this time of rest, and help me receive it as a gift.
!!bring my thoughts back to You!!, whenever they wander toward what does not come from You.
in Jesus' name, amen.

~ ↳ written by me
