purpose & providence | hands and feet
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, give me eyes to see how i can be the answer to Your prayer today.
show me how i can meet the needs of the people around me.
help me be the hands and feet of Jesus.
as i serve You, let the way i live inspire others to serve You too.
increase the number of people who love You by loving others.
in Jesus' name, amen.
