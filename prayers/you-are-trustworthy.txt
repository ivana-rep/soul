strength & trust | You are trustworthy
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

thank You, Jesus, because i can trust in You.
i can be secure in You.
secure that You love me.
secure that You see me.
secure that You have good plans for me.
when i start placing my trust elsewhere, bring my focus back to You and guide me to trust You more and more.
You are trustworthy!
in Jesus' name, amen.
