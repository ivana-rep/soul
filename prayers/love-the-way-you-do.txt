↳ [back to index](index.html)

Jesus, thank You for loving me regardless of the mistakes i've made.
because You love me, i want to love others even when they hurt me.
in moments when i find it hard to love someone, please encourage and strengthen me.
fill me with compassion and teach me to love others, just as You love them.
amen.
