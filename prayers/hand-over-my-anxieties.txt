worry & anxiety | hand over my anxieties
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God the Father, thank You for caring about every detail of my life.
forgive me when i spend so much mental energy worrying about circumstances i cannot control.
i surrender every pressing situation to You.
i ask You to act in miraculous ways.
thank You that i can hand over my anxieties to You and receive Your peace in return.
in Jesus' name i pray, amen.
