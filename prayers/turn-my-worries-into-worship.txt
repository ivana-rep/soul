God, i don't want to worry,
but sometimes it seems unavoidable.
will You help me to let go of the things i can't control?
teach me to turn my worries into worship.
fill my thoughts with Your peace.
i give every anxious thought to You!
today, show me opportunities to serve You.
in Jesus' name, amen.
