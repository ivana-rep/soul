Lord, before starting this prayer today
I come in front of You without masks,
because there are battles that no one sees and understands,
there are inner wars,
nights where the body is tired but thoughts do not rest,
and even if I smile in front of others You know the truth of my heart,
You know how much it's costing me.
there are times I think I can no longer continue like this.
my mind runs fast,
fear often visits me
and my thoughts are filled with scenarios that have never happened and may never even happen.
I am tired of living alarmed,
tired of fighting with myself,
tired of pretending I'm fine even though inside I'm collapsing,
but with You oh Lord,
I don't want to pretend.
today I come to You to abandon myself to You and Your presence,
because I know You don't refuse who is tired,
You do not ignore my labor,
You do not abandon who is hurt.
Lord, calm my anxieties, the inner noises, this storm that I am carrying in my heart,
and those concerns that seem bigger than my faith.
for today I choose to remind myself that You continue to be God even when I fear.
Father teach me to breathe slowly,
teach me how to wait and listen to Your voice,
so that it may sound louder in my ears more than my thoughts.
today I want to rest in You,
give me Your peace,
the one that does not pass not even in the face of wars.
