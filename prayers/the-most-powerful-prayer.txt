fear & courage | the most powerful prayer
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=prayers/la-preghiera-piu-potente.txt)

Lord, put the blood of Jesus over my children.
guard them where i cannot follow.
cover my home, my work, and every person tied to me.

wherever i go today, Your favor goes first.

whatever i have missed, Your goodness is still behind me, catching it.

every plan set quietly against my life, i hand it to You, and i refuse to be afraid of it.

!!not because i am strong,
because You are already there.!!

---

it's an important prayer because it covers more ground in a few lines than most prayers do in an hour.
it asks for protection, then it stops asking and starts trusting.
and after that, is the part most people never say, entrusting God with worries and difficulties.
we sometimes ask for protection we've already been given.
what you place under His care, He keeps.
