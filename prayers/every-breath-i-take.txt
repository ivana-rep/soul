gratitude | every breath i take
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, thank You for sending Your Son to take on the weight of my mistakes.
because of His sacrifice, i can experience Your heavenly peace on earth.
this week, remind me what Jesus accomplished on the cross
and the eternal life change i can experience because of His resurrection.
let me glorify You with every breath i take.
in Jesus' name, amen.
