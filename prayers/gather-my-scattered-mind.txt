Lord, You know how easily my mind wanders.
even when i open Your word, even when i pray, my thoughts drift away
before i notice, and i'm sorry for giving You only half of my attention.
i can't fix this on my own, so i bring it to You.
!!gather my scattered thoughts and quiet the noise inside me!!,
so that i can stay with You, one line, one moment at a time.
when i drift, don't let me be discouraged: gently bring me back,
and teach me to return to You without frustration, as many times as it takes.
let my time with You become the place where my mind finally rests.
amen.
