God, fill me with Your Spirit and guide me into truth.
help me to overcome my fears so Your truth can shine in the darkness.
forgive me when i resist Your wisdom and go my own way.
guide me into the freedom and joy that are found only in following Your path.
in Jesus' name, amen.
