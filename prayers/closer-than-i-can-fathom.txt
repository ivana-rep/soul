God, You know exactly what I'm going through.
You're not surprised or intimidated by it.
thank You for always being close; for standing by me through the good and bad.
You never fail me.
today, please remind me that !!You're right here—closer than I can fathom!!.
remind me that i have nothing to fear, and give me the courage to live boldly for You.
in Jesus' name, amen.
