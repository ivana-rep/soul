god,
when i look around me, i am amazed at all You have done and created. You created the leaves that blow in the wind, and the wind itself.
You made the sky and the trees—and yet You took the greatest delight in creating people like me. thank You for showing us endless grace and love. thank You for giving us life and purpose.
in Jesus' name, amen.
