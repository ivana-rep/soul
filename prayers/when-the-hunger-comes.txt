Lord, You are with me in this fast, and Your strength is enough for me.
when my body asks for food, help me stay steady and keep my eyes on You.
!!let every pang become a prayer!!, every craving a reminder that i don't live by bread alone.
guard me from complaining and from pride, and keep my heart light.
feed me with Your word, and carry me to the end of this time.
amen.
