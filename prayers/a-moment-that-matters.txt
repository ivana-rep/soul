God, i am humbled that You carefully consider my life.
even though i am only here on earth for a moment, You care deeply about that moment. when i really think about this, it humbles me. who am i, that the God of the universe cares about me? thank You for loving me. today, show me how to consider others like You have considered me. in Jesus' name, amen.
