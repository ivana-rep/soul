Jesus, You show me every day what unconditional love looks like.
Your sacrificial death opened the way for me to experience eternal life with You,
a life where suffering no longer exists.
no matter what i face, i want to live life with You.
so keep me close to You, and give me the strength to suffer willingly for You.
amen.
