gratitude | let my life be a song of praise
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

Composer of the cosmos, You fill the world with melody and meaning.
thank You for this song that stirs my heart, awakens my memories, and lifts my spirit.
as the notes fill my ears, may Your joy fill my soul.
let my life be a song of praise to You, singing truth in sorrow and gratitude in gladness.
amen.
