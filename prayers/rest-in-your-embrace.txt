love & grace | rest in Your embrace
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, thank You for Your tender mercy that longs to draw me close to You.
help me trust in Your love and receive the grace You give freely.
forgive me for having refused Your care, and teach me to rest in Your embrace.
may Your mercy transform my heart to reflect Your compassion.
amen.
