gratitude | You haven't abandoned me
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, today has been a difficult day.
perhaps i cried, perhaps i smiled on the outside whilst crumbling on the inside.
but i'm still here.
this tells me that You haven't abandoned me.
thank You.
not for what i've understood,
but what You've kept standing
when i could no longer hold on
