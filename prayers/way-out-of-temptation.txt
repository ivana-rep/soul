God, because i know Your Son Jesus, sin has no control over my life.
but, because the world is not yet fully redeemed, i am still tempted.
Your word says that You will provide a way out of temptation.
thank You!
please help me find the way out of temptation and give me the courage to say "no" to the things that could harm me.
i want to follow You faithfully in every moment.
in Jesus' name, amen.
