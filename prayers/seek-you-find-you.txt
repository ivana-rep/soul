seeking & finding | seek You, find You
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, it is when i seek You that i find You.
so, today, help me to draw near to You on purpose.
give me the courage to shift my focus from my circumstances to You.
i want to fully surrender to You, coming to You before everything else.
in Jesus' name, amen.
