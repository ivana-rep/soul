help me to close my eyes tonight knowing that tomorrow doesn't depend solely on me,
that there is someone who never sleeps, who knows me better than i know myself.
remind me tonight that i am not alone, because that is what frightens me most: feeling alone.
and You promised that will never happen, as Psalm 121 says.
the One who watches over you never sleeps.
