God, nothing is impossible for You.
You love me even when i'm unloveable.
You are good to me even when i don't deserve it.
You are kind and compassionate toward me even when i mess up.
You show me what !!faithful love!! looks like.
thank You for never leaving or forsaking me!
today, remind me of all the ways You have shown up in my life.
You are worthy of my worship.
in Jesus' name, amen.
