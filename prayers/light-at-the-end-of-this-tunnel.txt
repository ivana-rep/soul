God, from whom nothing is hidden, You know the depth of my pain, and every little way it's causing grief in my heart.
sometimes, i feel like i'm waiting in the dark. please equip a few good friends to also see and know me deeply — friends who can walk with me through this season. and as my strong refuge, protect me from losing sight of the light at the end of this dark tunnel. help me trust that You are near, even when i can't feel it. teach me to hope in Your word, and to believe that You will come through. amen.
