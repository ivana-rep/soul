seeking & finding | my deepest hunger
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, thank You for Jesus, the living bread that satisfies my deepest hunger.
teach me to seek what lasts: Your forgiveness, Your love and eternal life.
help me trust in Your providence and come to Your table with a heart open to Your grace.
fill me with Your life today.
amen.
