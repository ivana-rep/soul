greed & contentment | guard my heart
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

God, guard my heart against the endless craving for more.
help me to find contentment beyond possessions.
teach me to redirect my focus to seek Your Kingdom and righteousness above all else.
may my life reflect Your character and heart.
amen.
