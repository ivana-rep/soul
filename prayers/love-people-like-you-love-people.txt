God, even though we mess up, hurt each other, and offend You — You still love us.
You still have mercy on us. thank You.
forgive me for all the times i've chosen anger and bitterness over forgiveness and compassion.
please reveal who i need to show mercy to today, and then help me to obey Your prompting.
show me how to love people like You love people.
in Jesus' name, amen.
