Father, thank You for caring so deeply about who I am.
i am so grateful that one day You will take away all the pain and hardship i have lived through.
but until that day comes, i will trust in You and hold on to You.
i will place my hope in You.
i will bring my pain to You and let You comfort me.
in Jesus' name, amen.
