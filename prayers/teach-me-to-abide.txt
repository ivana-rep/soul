Lord, teach me to abide — not just in the morning, but all day.
let Your word stay in me through the ordinary moments.
speak through me when i don't have the words.
remind me i can do nothing apart from You, and everything when i stay close.
amen.

---

abiding isn't reserved for quiet mornings, it happens mid-task, during the school run, the dishes, brushing teeth.
the word doesn't wait for stillness to arrive first, it moves through whatever the day already is.
sometimes the answer comes not from preparation but from staying close enough that it's already there when needed.
