strength | You have overcome the world

God, i believe in You.
whatever happens, i believe that You have overcome the world.
even though difficulties are always present, You are my constant source of peace.
You surround me and protect me.
nothing is too difficult for You to accomplish.
i will praise You because You are the One i trust in: You are in control.
amen.
