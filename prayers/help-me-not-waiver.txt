God, i need You. You've given me everything i need to live a godly life, but that doesn't mean it's easy. please help me to not waiver in my faith. help me to be on guard against any attacks or lies from the devil. give me the courage to be strong and stand firm in my belief in You, and to live for You every day. in Jesus' name, amen.
