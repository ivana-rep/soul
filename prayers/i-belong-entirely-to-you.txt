strength | i belong entirely to You

heavenly Father, into Your loving hands i place my life, my heart, my family, my future, and every burden i carry.
i surrender my fears, my plans, my successes, and my failures to Your holy will.
remove from me everything that keeps me from You, and fill me with trust, peace, and unwavering faith.
teach me to accept Your will in all things, knowing that Your plans are always greater than my own.
strengthen me to follow You with a humble and obedient heart, no matter where You lead me.
!!i belong entirely to You, Father!!.
guide me, protect me, and use my life for Your glory.
through Jesus Christ, Your Son, and by the power of the Holy Spirit, i place my complete trust in You.
amen.
