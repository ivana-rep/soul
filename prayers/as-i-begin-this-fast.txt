Father, as i begin this fast, i set this time apart for You.
i'm not trying to earn anything, i just want to make room.
!!every time i feel hungry, turn my attention back to You!!.
humble me, quiet the noise, and show me what You want me to see.
let this stay between You and me, not something to show.
amen.
