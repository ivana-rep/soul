joy & presence | with me and within me
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

Lord, help me trust You in the depths of my being — that's where You live, in constant communion with me.
when I feel flustered and frazzled on the outside, let me not get upset with myself.
i am only human, and the swirl of events around me will sometimes feel overwhelming.
rather than scolding myself for my humanness, let me remember that You are both with me and within me.
You are with me at all times, encouraging and supportive, never condemning.
deep within me, where You live, let Your peace be my continual experience.
help me slow down my pace of living, and quiet my mind in Your presence,
so i can hear You bestow the resurrection blessing: peace be with me.

~ ↳ source: [reinterpretation of Jesus calling, july 31st](https://www.jesuscalling.com/)
