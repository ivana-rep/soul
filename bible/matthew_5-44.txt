[44] but i say, !!love your enemies!! pray for those who persecute you!

---

that's one of the hardest part of believing. following Christ teaching to love your enemies.
to love your enemies is not agreeing with them.
i don't have to understand someone, or share their view on life, money, relationships, religion, to love them.
love means giving first, considering others before myself, looking for what's good in them even when it's hard to find.
it doesn't wait for my feelings to cooperate. it asks for the choice.
that changes everything, changes the tone of every conversation, changes your point of view on the world. assuming even your enemy is worth of listening and consideration.
