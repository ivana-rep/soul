[24] the disciples went and woke Him up, shouting,
"master, master, we're going to drown!"
when Jesus woke up, He rebuked the wind and the raging waves,
and suddenly the storm stopped and all was calm.
[25] then He asked them, !!"where is your faith?"!!
the disciples were terrified and amazed.
"who is this man?" they asked each other.
"when He gives a command, even the wind and waves obey Him!"

---

it's not that having Jesus in the boat means the storm skips you.
the wind still comes, the water still fills in, the fear is still real while it's happening.
but He's in the boat the whole time, even asleep, even when it looks like He isn't doing anything about it.
the question isn't whether the storm will come. it's where my faith goes while I'm in it.
