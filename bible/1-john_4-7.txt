[7] dear friends, let us continue to love one another,
for !!love comes from God!!.
anyone who loves is a child of God and knows God.

---

love isn't love when i leave God out of it — it's just performance, or need, or fear dressed up nicely.
real love comes from somewhere, it doesn't originate in me.
if i'm loving well, it's because i'm drawing from a source bigger than my own patience or mood.
knowing God and loving people aren't two separate things — they're the same movement.
