[23] then Jesus got into the boat and started across the lake with His disciples.
[24] suddenly, a fierce storm struck the lake, with waves breaking into the boat.
but Jesus was sleeping.
[25] the disciples went and woke Him up, shouting, "Lord, save us! we're going to drown!"
[26] Jesus responded, "!!why are you afraid? you have so little faith!!"
then He got up and rebuked the wind and waves, and suddenly there was a great calm.
[27] the disciples were amazed. "who is this man?" they asked. "even the winds and waves obey Him!"

~ ↳ source: [Luke 8:22-25](post.html?p=bible/luke_8-22-25.txt)

---

it's not that having Jesus in the boat means the storm skips you.
the wind still comes, the water still fills in, the fear is still real while it's happening.
but He's in the boat the whole time, even asleep, even when it looks like He isn't doing anything about it.
the question isn't whether the storm will come. it's where my faith goes while I'm in it.
