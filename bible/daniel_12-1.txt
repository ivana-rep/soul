Daniel 12:1 | the archangel who stands guard
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

[1] at that time Michael, the archangel who stands guard over your nation, will arise.
!!then there will be a time of anguish greater than any since nations first came into existence.!!
but at that time every one of your people whose name is written in the book will be rescued.

---

Michael doesn't fight for territory or glory here — He arises specifically when His people face what the text calls anguish unlike any before. that's what guardianship looks like: not constant intervention, but presence exactly at the worst moment.
