[9] but even Michael, one of the mightiest of the angels, did not dare accuse the devil of blasphemy,
but simply said, "!!the Lord rebuke you!!"
(this took place when Michael was arguing with the devil about Moses' body.)

---

Michael had every right, and by some measures every power, to condemn the devil outright — instead He hands judgment back to God, and says nothing more than that. restraint, here, isn't weakness: it's knowing exactly whose authority it actually is.
