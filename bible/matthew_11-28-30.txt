[28] come to Me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens,
and I will give you rest.
[29] take My yoke upon you. let Me teach you,
because I am humble and gentle at heart,
and !!you will find rest for your souls!!.
[30] for My yoke is easy to bear,
and the burden I give you is light.

~ ↳ source: [sophie cunningham iga](https://www.tiktok.com/@donnastewart/video/7648116413546237198?lang=it-IT)

---

read this verse when you're feeling overwhelmed by the burdens of life.
learn how to give up control, and trust God will take care of you.
