[30] !!He must become greater and greater, and i must become less and less!!.

---

He doesn't just remove the envy — He increases His peace.
He doesn't just take the fear — He increases His courage.
He doesn't just decrease the pride — He increases His presence, until there's no room left for it to settle.
He must increase, and i must decrease. not as loss — as the only trade that actually works.
