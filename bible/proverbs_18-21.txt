[21] the tongue can bring death or life;
those who love to talk will reap the consequences.

---

words aren't neutral — they land somewhere, and what they build or break tends to outlast the moment they were said in. a careless sentence can wound for years; a well-placed one can carry someone through a bad day. Solomon doesn't call this a suggestion, he calls it a law: whatever you feed with your mouth, you'll eventually reap.
