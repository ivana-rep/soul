[1] praise the Lord, who is my rock.
He trains my hands for war and gives my fingers skill for battle.
[2] !!He is my loving ally and my fortress, my tower of safety, my rescuer!!.
He is my shield, and I take refuge in Him.
He makes the nations submit to me.

---

this verse is part of a three-part formation. psalm 91 is the defensive front — protection over you, your family, your home, your mindset. psalm 20 is the offensive front — a victory declared before the battle is even won. psalm 144 is the sword — hands trained for war, and a legacy that outlasts the fight itself. together, they cover what's needed to stand: shelter first, then a shout, then a weapon in hand.
