[14] Alexander the coppersmith did me much harm, but !!the Lord will judge him for what he has done.
[15] be careful of him!!, for he fought against everything we said.

~ ↳ source: [ben bennett, resolution movement](https://resolutionmovement.org)

---

Paul doesn't pretend it didn't happen: he did me much harm.
he doesn't take revenge either: the Lord will judge him.
and he doesn't open the door again: be careful of him.
forgiving isn't forgetting.
there's a big difference between remembering to punish and remembering to protect.
the first is revenge. the second can be wisdom.
you can hand the debt over to God and still keep your distance.
that doesn't make you bitter, or less of a Christian.
forgive, and remember wisely.
