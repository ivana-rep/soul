[3] !!You will keep in perfect peace!! all who trust in You,
all whose thoughts are fixed on You!
[4] trust in the Lord always,
for the Lord God is the eternal Rock.

---

when worries start to overwhelm you, imagine lifting up your eyes and seeing Jesus standing over you. nothing is too hard for Him to handle. a reminder to give Him whatever concerns come to mind.
