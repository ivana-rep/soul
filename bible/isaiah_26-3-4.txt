[3] !!You will keep in perfect peace!! all who trust in You,
all whose thoughts are fixed on You!
[4] trust in the Lord always,
for the Lord God is the eternal Rock.

---

when worries start to overwhelm you, imagine lifting up your eyes and seeing Jesus standing over you. 
nothing is too hard for Him to handle. a reminder to give Him whatever concerns come to mind.

the promise isn't tied to your situation. it's tied to your mind.
not the bank account, not the diagnosis, not the relationships — where your thoughts are fixed.
you keep trying to fix everything around you, when the only thing He asks you to fix is your focus.
maybe that's why you're so tired. why you're anxious, why you can't sleep.
your thoughts keep running ahead of God, and faith keeps pulling them back to Him.
the moment you trust what you see more than who He is, fear takes over.
He isn't asking you to live worried. He's calling you to live anchored — on the eternal Rock.
fix your mind on Him, stay there, and His peace will meet you there.
