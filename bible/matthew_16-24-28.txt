Matthew 16:24-28 | take up your cross

[24] then Jesus said to His disciples, "if any of you wants to be My follower,
you must give up your own way, !!take up your cross, and follow Me!!.
[25] if you try to hang on to your life, you will lose it.
but if you give up your life for My sake, you will save it.
[26] and what do you benefit if you gain the whole world but lose your own soul?
is anything worth more than your soul?
[27] for the Son of Man will come with His angels in the glory of His Father and will judge all people according to their deeds.
[28] and I tell you the truth, some standing here right now will not die before they see the Son of Man coming in His Kingdom."

[37] is anything worth more than your soul?
