[34] so don't worry about tomorrow,
for tomorrow will bring its own worries.
!!today's trouble is enough for today!!.

---

worry multiplies when i borrow tomorrow's trouble today.
one day at a time is not a cliché, it's a mercy.
today already has enough to carry, i don't need to add what hasn't happened yet.
God gives grace for today, not for the imagined version of tomorrow.
