[34] so don't worry about tomorrow,
for tomorrow will bring its own worries.
!!today's trouble is enough for today!!.

---

worry multiplies when i borrow tomorrow's trouble today.
prayer is where that trouble actually belongs — handed over instead of carried alone.
one day at a time is not a cliché, it's a mercy, and prayer is how i live it out instead of just repeating it to myself.
today already has enough to carry; what's left after i've done what i can, i bring to God in prayer instead of dragging it into tomorrow.
