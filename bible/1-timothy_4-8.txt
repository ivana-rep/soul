[8] physical training is good, but !!training for godliness is much better!!,
promising benefits in this life and in the life to come.

---

physical training is good — Paul doesn't say otherwise.
but it's not the finish line, it's practice for something bigger.
training for godliness uses the same muscles: discipline, patience, showing up on the hard days.
care for your body, but don't stop there — let it become training ground for training your soul too.
benefits in this life and in the life to come: that's the difference between a workout and a calling.
