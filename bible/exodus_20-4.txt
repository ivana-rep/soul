[4] "!!you must not make for yourself an idol!! of any kind
or an image of anything in the heavens or on the earth or in the sea.

---

God doesn't need you to carve Him into wood or metal.
He already made His image — you.
!!"in the image of God He created them"!! ([Genesis 1:27](post.html?p=bible/genesis_1-27.txt)).

an idol is an attempt to shrink the infinite into something you can hold, control, bow to on your own terms.
but the truest image of God was never a statue.
it's the neighbor in front of you.

so this command isn't only about statues.
it's about where you go looking for God — not something you build, but someone you already are, standing in front of someone else who is too.
