[4] remain in me, and I will remain in you.
for a branch cannot produce fruit if it is severed from the vine,
and !!you cannot be fruitful unless you remain in me!!.

---

read this verse to remind yourself to pause, to remain still, for it is in stillness that you can abide in Christ.
only by abiding in Christ you can truly express yourself and your potential.
