[16] now may the Lord of peace Himself give you His peace at all times and in every situation.
!!the Lord be with you all!!.

~ ↳ source: [Jesus calling, august 7th](https://www.jesuscalling.com/)

---

peace that depends on figuring everything out never lasts.
one problem gets solved, another pops up.
real peace isn't found in mastery or understanding.
it's given, by the Lord, at all times, in every situation.
