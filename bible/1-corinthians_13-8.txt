[8] prophecy and speaking in unknown languages and special knowledge will become useless.
but !!love will last forever!!.

---

paul doesn't hedge here — prophecy, tongues, even knowledge itself all have an expiration date built into them, but love will last forever. everything else on that list was given for a season; love wasn't.

worth being precise about what that means. it isn't a test to run backward on people — "if it fails, it wasn't real love" turns a promise into a verdict, which isn't what paul is doing. he isn't diagnosing failed relationships; he's naming what outlasts everything that eventually runs out.

so hold onto the promise as it's actually written: love doesn't wear out. whatever collapses around it, it isn't love that quits.
