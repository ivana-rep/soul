[12] for you bless the godly, o Lord;
!!you surround them with your shield of love!!.

---

envy, comparison, jealousy — they don't turn off His favor. they just talk me out from under it.
His shield doesn't move. i'm the one who steps out from under it, trading His covering for the exhausting work of keeping score against people who weren't even competing.
so today i stay under the shield. i let Him surround me instead of measuring myself against everyone else's version of blessed.
