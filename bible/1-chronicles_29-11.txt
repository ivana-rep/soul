[11] Yours, O Lord, is the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory, and the majesty.
everything in the heavens and on earth is Yours, O Lord, and this is Your kingdom.
!!we adore You as the one who is over all things!!.

---

don't tell God how big your storm is. tell the storm how big your God is.
His is the greatness, the power, the glory — not maybe, not sometimes, always.
everything in heaven and on earth already belongs to Him, including whatever is shaking your ground right now.
i keep forgetting the scale. He's not squeezed into the size of my problem — my problem fits inside Him.
