[7] though i am surrounded by troubles,
You will protect me from the anger of my enemies.
You reach out Your hand,
and the power of Your right hand saves me.
[8] !!the Lord will work out His plans for my life!!—
for Your faithful love, O Lord, endures forever.
don't abandon me, for You made me.

~ ↳ source: [tiktok, @johnstoneism](https://www.tiktok.com/@johnstoneism/video/7674059087444823327?_r=1&_t=ZN-994TgCK5NPK)

---

a perfect plan doesn't always feel like one.

heartache, betrayal, pain — not detours. the price of being here at all.

every search for love that lands in the wrong place is still aimed at God, just misfiled.

the way through isn't doing more. it's surrendering more. laying down the weapons.
