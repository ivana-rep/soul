[8] we think you ought to know, dear brothers and sisters,
about the trouble we went through in the province of Asia.
we were crushed and overwhelmed beyond our ability to endure,
and we thought we would never live through it.
[9] in fact, we expected to die.
but as a result, !!we stopped relying on ourselves and learned to rely only on God!!,
who raises the dead.

---

even Paul was crushed beyond what he could handle. he says it plainly — beyond our ability to endure.
that's not a failure of faith, it's the exact place faith usually starts.
i keep trying to prove i can hold it together on my own, like that's the point.
but the breaking wasn't the end of the story for him — it was where he stopped relying on himself and started relying on God.
