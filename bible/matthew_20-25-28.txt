[25] but Jesus called them together and said,
"you know that the rulers in this world lord it over their people,
and officials flaunt their authority over those under them.
[26] but among you it will be different.
whoever wants to be a leader among you must be your servant,
[27] and whoever wants to be first among you must become your slave.
[28] for even the Son of Man !!came not to be served but to serve others!!
and to give His life as a ransom for many."
