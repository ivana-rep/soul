[14] "if you keep quiet at a time like this,
deliverance and relief for the Jews will arise from some other place,
but you and your relatives will die.
who knows if perhaps you were made queen !!for just such a time as this!!?"
[15] then Esther sent this reply to Mordecai:
[16] "go and gather together all the Jews of Susa and fast for me.
do not eat or drink for three days, night or day.
my maids and i will do the same.
and then, though it is against the law, i will go in to see the king.
!!if i must die, i must die!!."
