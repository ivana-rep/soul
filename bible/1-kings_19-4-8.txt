[4] then he went on alone into the wilderness, traveling all day.
he sat down under a solitary broom tree and prayed that he might die.
"!!i have had enough, Lord!!," he said.
"take my life, for i am no better than my ancestors who have already died."
[5] then he lay down and slept under the broom tree.
but as he was sleeping, an angel touched him and told him, "get up and eat!"
[6] he looked around and there beside his head was some bread baked on hot stones and a jar of water!
so he ate and drank and lay down again.
[7] then the angel of the Lord came again and touched him and said,
"get up and eat some more, or the journey ahead will be too much for you."
[8] so he got up and ate and drank, and the food gave him enough strength
to travel forty days and forty nights to Mount Sinai, the mountain of God.
