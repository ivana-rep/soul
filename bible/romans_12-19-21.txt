Romans 12:19-21 | never take revenge, conquer evil with good
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=bible/romans_12-19-21.txt)

[19] dear friends, !!never take revenge.
leave that to the righteous anger of God!!.
for the scriptures say, 'I will take revenge; I will pay them back,' says the Lord.
[20] instead, 'if your enemies are hungry, feed them.
if they are thirsty, give them something to drink.
in doing this, you will heap burning coals of shame on their heads.'
[21] don't let evil conquer you, but !!conquer evil by doing good!!.

~ ↳ source: [ben bennett, resolution movement](https://resolutionmovement.org)

---

forgive, no matter how hard it is. it is not a task for us, it is God's right.
that doesn't mean doing nothing — feed the one who wronged you, meet the enemy's need instead of matching their harm.
don't let evil have the last word. answer it with good, and evil loses its grip.
forgiving isn't forgetting.
it hands the debt over to God, without pretending the wrong wasn't real.
it doesn't erase the memory, the boundaries, or the need to heal.
forgiveness hands over the debt. wisdom remembers the lesson. healing restores the heart.
