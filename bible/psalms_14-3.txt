[3] but no, all have turned away;
all have become corrupt.
!!no one does good, not a single one!!

---

psalm 14 opens with "the fool says in his heart, there is no God" — this verse isn't singling anyone out as especially wicked, it's a diagnosis of the human condition left to itself: no one, on their own, does what is genuinely good.
Paul quotes it almost word for word in Romans 3 to make the same case to everyone, not just the obviously corrupt.
it isn't the whole psalm's conclusion though — it still ends looking toward God restoring what's broken. the honesty here just comes first: no one earns their own rescue.
