[21] and there by the Ahava Canal, i gave orders for all of us to fast and humble ourselves before our God.
we prayed that He would give us a safe journey and protect us, our children, and our goods as we traveled.
[22] for i was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to accompany us and protect us from enemies along the way.
after all, we had told the king, "our God's hand of protection is on all who worship Him, but His fierce anger rages against those who abandon Him."
[23] so we fasted and earnestly prayed that our God would take care of us, and !!He heard our prayer!!.
