[4] show me the right path, o Lord;
point out the road for me to follow.
[5] !!lead me by Your truth and teach me!!,
for You are the God who saves me.
all day long I put my hope in You.

---

it's okay to not have it all figured out, God doesn't expect that from us.
ask for guidance, and have the patience to wait and hope for God's intervention, at the right time.
