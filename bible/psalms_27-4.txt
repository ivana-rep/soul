[4] the one thing I ask of the Lord —
the thing I seek most —
is to live in the house of the Lord all the days of my life,
!!delighting in the Lord's perfections and meditating in His Temple!!.

~ ↳ source: [Jesus calling, august 16th](https://www.jesuscalling.com/)

---

seeking God's face isn't a chore i'm supposed to check off before the day starts.
it's what happens when the desire itself is real, not performed.
David doesn't ask for one thing because it's the responsible request — he asks because it's what he actually wants most.
i want that to be true of me too, not just the discipline of showing up, but the wanting underneath it.
