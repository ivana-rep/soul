[23] !!guard your heart above all else, for it determines the course of your life!!.

---

this verse isn't about suppressing what you feel, it's about paying attention to what you let take root. what fills your heart eventually shapes what you do — a grudge, a comparison, an unspoken hurt, left unchecked, quietly steer your choices before you even notice. guarding it means naming what's actually there and bringing it to God, instead of letting it sit.
