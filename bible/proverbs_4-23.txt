[23] !!guard your heart above all else, for it determines the course of your life!!.

---

there's a box in front of me, and i keep tossing thoughts into it — worries, comparisons, replayed conversations, things i didn't say.
some days it's overflowing, and i don't even notice what's collecting in there.
but this verse doesn't say manage your thoughts. it says guard your heart, above all else.
because what i let sit in that box quietly reroutes the course of my life without me ever choosing it directly.
so today: i open the box, i look inside, i talk to Him about what's actually there — instead of shutting the lid and hoping it settles on its own.
