[1] those who live in the shelter of the Most High !!will find rest in the shadow of the Almighty!!.

---

this verse is part of a three-part formation. psalm 91 is the defensive front — protection over you, your family, your home, your mindset. [psalm 20](post.html?p=bible/psalms_20-4-7.txt) is the offensive front — sung before the battle, declaring victory in advance because the outcome already rests with God. [psalm 144](post.html?p=bible/psalms_144-1-2.txt) is the sword — hands trained for war, ready before the fight begins. together, they cover what's needed to stand: shelter first, then a shout, then a weapon in hand.
