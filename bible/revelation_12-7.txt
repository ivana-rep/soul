[7] then there was war in heaven.
!!Michael and his angels fought against the dragon and his angels.!!

---

this is the only scene in scripture where Michael's role is purely military — everywhere else He argues, guards, or defers. here, the fight is unavoidable: some claims can't be negotiated with, only resisted.
