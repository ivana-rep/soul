Isaiah 43:1 | do not be afraid, I've called you by name, you are mine
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

[1] but now, o Jacob, listen to the Lord who created you.
o Israel, the one who formed you says,
"!!do not be afraid, for I have ransomed you.
I have called you by name; you are mine.!!"

---

hey you, yes you — stop for a second. God has some words for you, my child.
"[before I formed you in the womb, I knew you](post.html?p=bible/jeremiah_1-5.txt)."
"you are my beloved, and in you [I am well pleased. I have chosen you](post.html?p=bible/matthew_3-17.txt)."
"[I have loved you with an everlasting love](post.html?p=bible/jeremiah_31-3.txt). do you not perceive it?"
"for I know well [the plans I have for you — plans for your good, not for woe, to give you a future full of hope](post.html?p=bible/jeremiah_29-11.txt)."
"[when you call to me, I will listen to you](post.html?p=bible/jeremiah_29-12.txt); [when you seek me, you will find me](post.html?p=bible/jeremiah_29-13.txt)."
"for I am with you. [I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you](post.html?p=bible/isaiah_41-10.txt), [I will fight for you](post.html?p=bible/exodus_14-14.txt)."
"and so, my beloved — come to me. [return to me with all your heart](post.html?p=bible/joel_2-12.txt)."
"for behold, [I am with you always](post.html?p=bible/matthew_28-20.txt)."
"do not be afraid. I've called you by name — you are mine."
