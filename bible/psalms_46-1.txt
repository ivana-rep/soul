[1] God is our refuge and strength,
!!always ready to help in times of trouble!!.

---

not after the storm — in it. that's the part i keep missing.
i want proof He was there once it's over, some clean before-and-after.
but the help isn't waiting on the other side of the trouble, it's already inside it with me.
refuge isn't an escape from the storm. it's a person, standing in it, holding on to me while it passes.
