[16] but Jesus often withdrew to the wilderness for prayer.

---

there's a strange detail here — Jesus, with people constantly needing Him, still made room to disappear. not despite the demand, but because of it. withdrawal wasn't neglect of the mission, it was what sustained it. if He needed emptied space to stay full, the assumption that we can skip that step and still have anything left to give doesn't hold up.
