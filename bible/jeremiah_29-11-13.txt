[11] "!!for I know the plans I have for you!!," says the Lord.
"they are plans for good and not for disaster,
to give you a future and a hope."
[12] !!in those days when you pray, I will listen.!!
[13] if you look for Me wholeheartedly,
!!you will find Me!!.

~ ↳ source: [fernando woes iga](https://www.instagram.com/fernando.woes/)

---

i don't need the whole plan. i need to know someone holds it.
the anxiety was never really about the unknown — it was about carrying it alone.
He already knows: plans for good, not for disaster. that's not a small detail, that's the ground I'm standing on.
letting go of figuring it out. that was never my job to begin with.
