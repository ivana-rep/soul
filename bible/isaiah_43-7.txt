[7] bring all who claim Me as their God,
for !!I have made them for My glory!!.
it was I who created them.

---

i was made for God first — everything else, every relationship, every belonging, comes after that.
not because people don't matter, but because i can't actually love well from an empty identity.
His glory isn't a rival to my worth, it's the source of it.
made for Him, then for everyone else — that order isn't a demotion, it's the only foundation that holds.
