Esau got wealth, power, a nation of his own — and God never touched his life.
Jacob got wrestled with, corrected, refused permission to stay a deceiver.
he limped into the promised land, changed. Esau strolled through his exactly as he was.
that's the trade: ease or discipline. rarely both.
if He's correcting you, that's not distance. that's sonship.
