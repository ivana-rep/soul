Psalms 20:4-7 | the Lord rescues His anointed king
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=bible/psalms_20-4-7.txt)

[4] may He grant your heart's desires and make all your plans succeed.
[5] may we shout for joy when we hear of your victory
and raise a victory banner in the name of our God.
may the Lord answer all your prayers.
[6] !!now I know that the Lord rescues His anointed king.
He will answer him from His holy Heaven and rescue him by His great power!!.
[7] some nations boast of their chariots and horses,
but we boast in the name of the Lord our God.

---

this verse is part of a three-part formation. [psalm 91](post.html?p=bible/psalms_91-1.txt) * is the defensive front — protection over you, your family, your home, your mindset. psalm 20 is the offensive front — sung before the battle, declaring victory in advance because the outcome already rests with God. [psalm 144](post.html?p=bible/psalms_144-1-2.txt) * is the sword — hands trained for war, ready before the fight begins. together, they cover what's needed to stand: shelter first, then a shout, then a weapon in hand.
