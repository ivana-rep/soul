[23] !!search me, O God, and know my heart;
test me and know my anxious thoughts!!.

---

David isn't asking God to confirm what he already believes about himself — he's asking God to search past that, into the anxious thoughts he might not even say out loud. that kind of invitation takes trust: that whatever gets found isn't there to condemn, it's there to lead somewhere better.
