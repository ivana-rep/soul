[1] faith shows the reality of what we hope for;
!!it is the evidence of things we cannot see!!.
[2] through their faith, the people in days of old earned a good reputation.
[3] !!by faith we understand!! that the entire universe was formed at God's command,
that what we now see did not come from anything that can be seen.

---

faith shows the reality of what we hope for, it's not blind, it's substance before sight.
the ancients weren't commended for what they could prove, they were commended for what they trusted.
by faith we understand the universe was formed at God's word, the visible came from the invisible, not the other way around.
faith isn't the opposite of understanding, it's the foundation of it, some things are established before they're proven by sight.
believing first isn't a leap away from reason, it's what gives reason solid ground to stand on.
