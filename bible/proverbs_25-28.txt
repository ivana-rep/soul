[28] a person without self-control is like !!a city with broken-down walls!!.

---

self-control isn't a cage, it's a wall.
take it down and everything gets in — cravings, excess, whatever wants to move in and take over.
a city without walls isn't free, it's defenseless.
that's the body without discipline: exposed, not liberated.
guarding what you eat, drink, do to your body isn't legalism, it's keeping the walls standing — on something that was never fully yours to let fall.
