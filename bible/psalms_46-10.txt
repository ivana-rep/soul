[10] !!be still, and know that I am God!!! i will be honored by every nation. i will be honored throughout the world.

~ ↳ source: [tiktok @lucymvhf19i](https://www.tiktok.com/@lucymvhf19i)

---

"be still" is not a suggestion, it's an instruction — stop reaching for control you were never given.
you weren't listening to this by accident. this is the confirmation you asked for.
He hasn't taken His eyes off you — not through the finances, not through the health scare, not through what's happening in your family.
He's covering you with peace today. releasing healing. giving you strength for as long as you keep standing.
this hard place isn't the end of the story — it's the part before it turns beautiful.
a hundred thoughts can run through your mind about what could go wrong. the God who brought you this far isn't done.
He's shielding you from what you can't see, holding you in a hand you can't feel yet but are already inside.
breathe. this isn't your ending — it's the middle of a miracle.
keep trusting, keep obeying. He's the one removing what needs to go and opening what you couldn't open yourself.
push through the fear, the delay, the silence — he who endures to the end will be saved.
