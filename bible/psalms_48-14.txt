[14] for that is what God is like. He is !!our God forever and ever!!,
and He will guide us until we die.

~ ↳ source: [Jesus calling, august 14th](https://www.jesuscalling.com/)

---

everything around us keeps shifting — the world, our own bodies, even what science promises us. but God doesn't change with any of it. He was our God before, He's our God now, and He'll still be our God after we're gone. that's not a small thing to hold onto: the one guiding us today is the same one who will guide us until the very end, and beyond it.
