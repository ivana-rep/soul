[34] so now i am giving you a new commandment:
!!love each other. just as I have loved you!!,
you should love each other.

---

a new commandment, not a suggestion.
love each other, not just the easy ones, everyone.
the standard isn't how much i feel like loving someone, it's how Jesus loved.
He gave first. He didn't wait to be understood before choosing to love.
that's the model, not agreement, not comfort, love as the evidence of what i actually believe.
