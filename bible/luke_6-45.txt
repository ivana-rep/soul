Luke 6:45 | what you say flows from what is in your heart
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

[45] a good person produces good things from the treasury of a good heart,
and an evil person produces evil things from the treasury of an evil heart.
!!what you say flows from what is in your heart!!.

---

do you call yourself a follower of Jesus? prove it by living your life in a way that reflects His heart.
this doesn't mean you'll be perfect, but it does mean that even when you mess up, you will still try to make things right. and not because you must work for God's favor or earn His love, but because your actions reveal what is happening inside of you.
