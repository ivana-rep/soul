[4] but Jesus told him, "no! the scriptures say,
!!'people do not live by bread alone,
but by every word that comes from the mouth of God!!.'"

---

Jesus didn't deny that bread matters, He just refused to let it be the whole story.
the tempter offered a shortcut through the body, He answered through the word.
what actually holds a person together isn't only what feeds the stomach, it's what's spoken by God and trusted before it's proven.
