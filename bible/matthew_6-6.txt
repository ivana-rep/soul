[6] but !!when you pray, go away by yourself, shut the door behind you,
and pray to your Father in private.
then your Father, who sees everything, will reward you!!.

---

the loudest prayers aren't always the realest ones.
some of my truest conversations with God happen with no one else in the room.
closed door, no performance, no audience — just Him and me.
that's where He meets me. that's where He rewards me.
