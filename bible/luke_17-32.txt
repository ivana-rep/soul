[32] !!remember what happened to Lot's wife!!.

~ ↳ source: [ruvim](https://www.tiktok.com/@officialruvim/video/7635062569983544589)

---

lot's wife had one command: don't look back. she looked, and she turned into a pillar of salt — frozen on the spot, never entering the promise she was walking toward.
the end times aren't really the point. the point is: you can't move forward while still glancing at what you were delivered from. the sin you keep flirting with, the idols God freed you from — looking back at them is what stalls a life with Him.
her heart never fully left Sodom. that's the warning: obedience isn't only leaving, it's not turning around.
