[16] so i say, let the Holy Spirit guide your lives.
then you won't be doing what your sinful nature craves.

---

the fight against a craving is exhausting when it's fought head-on — Paul offers a different strategy here: don't resist the flesh, follow the Spirit instead. desire doesn't need to be white-knuckled into submission if something stronger is leading the way. so the daily question isn't just what am i resisting, but what am i walking toward.
