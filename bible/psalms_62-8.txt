[8] !!o my people, trust in Him at all times.
pour out your heart to Him, for God is our refuge!!.

~ ↳ source: [Jesus calling, august 15th](https://www.jesuscalling.com/)

---

trust in Him at all times isn't a mood, it's a rhythm — something you return to after a bad morning ambushes you, not just before it.
every interruption i get annoyed by is another invitation to talk to Him about it instead of pushing through alone.
i keep waiting to be composed enough to pray, but pouring out your heart doesn't require it being tidy first.
refuge isn't the absence of trouble, it's Him, staying with me inside it.
