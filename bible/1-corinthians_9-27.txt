1 Corinthians 9:27 | discipline my body like an athlete
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

[27] !!i discipline my body like an athlete, training it to do what it should.!!
otherwise, i fear that after preaching to others i myself might be disqualified.

---

Paul doesn't train for vanity, he trains like an athlete because he means what he preaches.
a body left undisciplined can undo everything the mouth says.
so he treats it the way an athlete treats theirs: with intention, not indulgence.
your body isn't separate from your witness — how you care for it says something true or something false about what you actually believe.
training it isn't punishment, it's taking seriously what was bought at a price.
