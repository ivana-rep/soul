[7] but blessed are those who !!trust in the Lord!! and have made the Lord their hope and confidence.
[8] they are like trees planted along a riverbank, with roots that reach deep into the water.
such trees are not bothered by the heat or worried by long months of drought.
their leaves stay green, and they never stop producing fruit.
[9] !!the human heart is the most deceitful of all things!!,
and desperately wicked. who really knows how bad it is?
[10] but I, the Lord, search all hearts and examine secret motives.
I give all people their due rewards, according to what their actions deserve.

---

the verse underpinning the argument in [Proverbs 3:5](post.html?p=bible/proverbs_3-5-6.txt) *: if the heart is the least reliable thing on which to rely, then "do not rely on your own understanding" is not humiliation but clarity. not pessimism — theological realism regarding the intrinsic limitations of human self-awareness.
