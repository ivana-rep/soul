[5] for his anger lasts only a moment, but his favor lasts a lifetime!
!!weeping may last through the night, but joy comes with the morning!!.

---

grief doesn't announce an end date. the night it moves through can feel indefinite, like morning is a rumor rather than a promise. david doesn't pretend the weeping isn't real — he places it right next to the truth that outlasts it: weeping may last through the night, but joy comes with the morning.

the two aren't in competition. the night is allowed to be as dark as it is; it just isn't the whole story. His favor outlasts His anger, and morning outlasts the night, every time.

so keep going. the place you're crying in now won't stay a place of tears — and you're not walking through it alone.
