[1] watch out! !!don't do your good deeds publicly, to be admired by others,
for you will lose the reward from your Father in Heaven!!.

---

there's a difference between doing good and being seen doing good.
one is for Him. the other is for an audience.
the moment i perform for approval, i've already collected my reward — applause, not Heaven.
i don't need anyone to notice. He already does.
