[2] don't copy the behavior and customs of this world,
but !!let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think!!.
then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.

---

transformation isn't subtraction. it's replacement.
i can't just empty myself out — a heart with nothing in it doesn't stay empty. whatever i stop feeding gets replaced by something else anyway.
so the question was never just what to remove. it's what to let renew my mind instead — new thoughts, new focus, new attention, His word louder than the noise.
