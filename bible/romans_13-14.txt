[14] instead, clothe yourself with the presence of the Lord Jesus Christ.
and don't let yourself think about ways to indulge your evil desires.

---

Paul doesn't say fight your desires directly — he says put on Christ instead. clothing isn't something you fight your way into, it's something you choose to wear. the same logic works with temptation: the fastest way to stop feeding a desire isn't staring it down, it's filling that space with something else entirely.
