!!the Lord gives His people strength.
the Lord blesses them with peace.!!

---

this psalm spends most of itself describing a storm — thunder over the water, lightning splitting cedars, the wilderness shaking.
and then it ends here, almost quietly. not with the storm calming, but with what the storm reveals: the same voice behind the thunder is the one handing out strength and peace to His own.
i don't need the noise to stop to receive that. He's not less present in the loud part.
