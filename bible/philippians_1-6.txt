[6] and I am certain that !!God, who began the good work within you, will continue his work until it is finally finished!! on the day when Christ Jesus returns.

---

the temptation halfway through anything hard is to assume God brought you this far just to watch you stall out here. but paul isn't describing a project God abandons partway — he's certain the one who started this will keep going until it's finished.

that certainty isn't wishful thinking, it's rooted in who's doing the work. an artist doesn't lay down the brush mid-stroke and walk away; the God who began something in you isn't in the business of leaving it half-built either.

so if it feels like you've stalled, that isn't proof He's finished with you. it's proof the work isn't finished yet.
