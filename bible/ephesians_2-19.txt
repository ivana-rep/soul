[19] so now you Gentiles are no longer strangers and foreigners.
you are citizens along with all of God's holy people.
!!you are members of God's family!!.

---

unloved, unimportant, insecure — those feelings all trace back to the same question: do I actually belong anywhere, to anyone? paul's answer is unambiguous: you are no longer a stranger or a foreigner, you're a citizen among God's own people, a member of His family.

family membership isn't earned by performance and it isn't revoked by a bad day. it's a status, not a mood — which is exactly why it holds steady on the days you don't feel it.

so on the days the feeling says otherwise, the fact doesn't change: you belong to Him, and that was settled long before the feeling ever showed up.
