[17] He existed before anything else,
and !!He holds all creation together!!.

---

Jesus is not an accessory to our life, a genie that makes life easier for us. He is the One who defines and sustains it.
when we allow Christ to become the lens through which we see our relationships, work, identity, and decisions, we begin to understand life as it truly is, not merely as it feels.
this shifts us from unstable, emotion-driven interpretation to a worldview anchored in the eternal nature of Christ.
