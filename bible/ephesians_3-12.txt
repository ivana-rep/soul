[12] because of Christ and our faith in Him, we can now come !!boldly and confidently!! into God's presence.

~ ↳ source: [ally yost, christ with coffee on ice](https://www.tiktok.com/@maybenotlilli/video/7668366084419882253)

---

a lot of people's relationship with God gets shaped by their relationship with people. if you've ever felt like a burden, too much, not enough — it's easy to project that onto Him too. you start approaching Him like someone trying not to get kicked out: apologizing for taking up space, hiding parts of yourself, assuming His love fluctuates with your latest performance.
but family doesn't work that way. a little child running into her father's room at 2am doesn't stop to ask if she's been good enough to deserve entry. she just comes, because she knows where she belongs. that's the confidence Paul is describing — not earned, just given.
