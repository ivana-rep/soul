Isaiah 6:8 | here i am, send me
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

[8] then i heard the Lord asking,
"whom should I send as a messenger to this people?
who will go for us?"
i said, "!!here i am. send me.!!"

---

isaiah sees the Lord on the throne, seraphim crying "holy, holy, holy," the foundations of the temple shaking.
his first reaction isn't awe — it's terror. "woe is me," he says, "i am a man of unclean lips."
a seraph touches his mouth with a burning coal from the altar. his guilt is taken away, his sin atoned for.
only then does he hear the Lord asking, "whom should I send? who will go for us?"
he says yes before he knows the assignment — the next verse is a hard message to deliver, to a people who won't listen.
the readiness comes after the encounter, not before it.
