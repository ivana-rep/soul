[4] always be full of joy in the Lord.
i say it again—rejoice!
[5] let everyone see that you are considerate in all you do.
remember, the Lord is coming soon.
[6] !!don't worry about anything; instead, pray about everything.!!
tell God what you need, and thank Him for all He has done.
[7] then you will experience God's peace, which exceeds anything we can understand.
His peace will guard your hearts and minds as you live in Christ Jesus.

---

joy is not a spontaneous feeling but a constant choice.
a direct antidote to anxiety: not denying the problem, but finding refuge in God who is near.
an encouragement to live life with joy, to trust in God and in the fact that He looks after us, and not to take everything too seriously.
