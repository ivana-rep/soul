[22] the smallest family will become a thousand people,
and the tiniest group will become a mighty nation.
!!at the right time!!, I, the LORD, will make it happen.

---

read this verse every time you feel that modern society is pushing you.
not everything is supposed to happen now, when you want it to happen.
trust in God, He knows when the right time is, you have to confide in Him, and trust His plan.
instant gratification is a scam, and it's what makes us so unhappy lately.
