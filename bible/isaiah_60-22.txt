!!At the right time!!, I, the LORD, will make it happenHume treated likeability as a set of practices, not a trait you either have or lack: yielding space to other people's egos, carrying things lightly, genuine modesty, reading a room and adjusting your register, minding how much space you take up in someone else's presence.

- - -

Read this verse every time you feel that modern society is pushing you. 
Not everything is supposed to happen now, when you want it to happen.
Trust in God, he knows when the right time is, you have to confide in Him, and trust His plan.
Instang gratification is a scam, and it's what makes us so unhappy lately.
