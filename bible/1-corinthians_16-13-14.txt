[13] be on guard. stand firm in the faith.
!!be courageous. be strong.!!
[14] and do everything with love.

---

paul wrote this to a church tearing itself apart — power, politics, division, immorality tolerated instead of confronted.
being on guard means staying awake to the lies that creep in when a community stops watching itself.
standing firm isn't stubbornness. it's refusing to let people-pleasing replace obedience to God.
courage and strength are for building each other up, not for winning arguments.
none of it means anything without love. strength without love turns into self-righteousness. courage without love turns into arrogance.
