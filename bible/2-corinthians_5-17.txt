[17] !!this means that anyone who belongs to Christ has become a new person.
the old life is gone; a new life has begun!!!

---

if nothing changed, i have to ask what actually happened.
belonging to Christ isn't a label added on top of the same life — it's a different life.
the old habits, the old fears, the old way of measuring myself, none of it gets to stay just because it's familiar.
new doesn't mean perfect. it means the direction changed, and i'm not who i was.
