[2] always be humble and gentle.
be patient with each other,
!!making allowance for each other's faults because of your love!!.

---

it's easy to slide into self-righteousness once you think you know the truth and someone else doesn't.
looking down on people, being harsh instead of gentle — it feels justified in the moment.
but the gospel doesn't leave room for that. all of us started at the same point, saved by grace, not by being right.
so patience with each other's faults isn't optional extra credit. it's just what love does when it remembers where it came from.
