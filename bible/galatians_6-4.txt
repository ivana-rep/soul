[4] pay careful attention to your own work, for then you will get the satisfaction of a job well done,
and !!you won't need to compare yourself to anyone else!!.

---

envy gets loud when someone else gets the thing i've been praying for.
comparison gets loud with one glance at a life that looks better than mine.
jealousy gets loud when credit goes to someone else for something i did quietly, months ago.
insecurity gets loud at 11pm when the house is quiet and my thoughts aren't.
the fix isn't pretending i don't feel it — it's noticing it, naming it, and bringing it to Him instead of feeding it.
pay attention to my own work. that's where the satisfaction was always going to come from, not from the comparison.
