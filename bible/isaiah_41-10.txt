[10] !!don't be afraid, for I am with you!!.
don't be discouraged, for I am your God.
I will strengthen you and help you.
I will hold you up with My victorious right hand.

---

there will always be trials and tribulations in life.
God will carry you through every storm in your life, and give you strength to make it, even when you feel like you can't.
