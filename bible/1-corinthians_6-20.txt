[20] for God bought you with a high price.
!!so you must honor God with your body.!!

---

your body isn't fully your own, it's been created in God's image, and it was bought at a high price.
imagine Jesus' arms outstretched as He dies for you.
now, imagine Jesus' arms outstretched as He invites you into a relationship with Him.
that's the price. that's why we should treat our bodies as temples.
in context, Paul's addressing sexual immorality (the body as temple of the Holy Spirit).
but i also see it in a broader "physical health & self-care" reading.
how we treat our bodies isn't separate from faith, it's part of it. taking care of your health is one quiet way of saying thank You for what God paid.
