[20] for God bought you with a high price.
!!so you must honor God with your body.!!

---

your body isn't fully your own — it was bought at a high price, so how you treat it matters.
sleep, food, movement, rest: these aren't separate from your faith, they're part of it.
taking care of your health is one quiet way of saying thank You for what God paid.
