[24] He personally carried our sins in His body on the cross
so that we can be dead to sin and live for what is right.
!!by His wounds you are healed!!.

---

the wounds aren't only the suffering — they're named as the mechanism of healing itself: by His wounds, not despite them. suffering carried by one person so another doesn't have to is the oldest shape of love there is.
