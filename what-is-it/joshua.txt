the sixth book of the bible, traditionally attributed to Joshua — Moses's successor, chosen to lead Israel across the Jordan and into the promised land after Moses's death. Old Testament, historical narrative.
tells the story of the conquest of Canaan, the fall of Jericho, and the division of the land among the twelve tribes. main themes: courage, God's faithfulness to His promises, and covenant obedience in a new land.
