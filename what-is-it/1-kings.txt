the eleventh book of the bible, part of a larger historical account traditionally grouped with 1-2 Samuel and 2 Kings, covering the united and then divided kingdom of Israel. Old Testament, historical narrative.
opens with Solomon's reign and the building of the temple, then follows the kingdom's split into Israel and Judah and the kings who ruled each — including the prophet Elijah's confrontations with idolatry. main themes: faithfulness and apostasy, God's covenant with David's line, and the cost of turning away from God.
