the first book of the bible, traditionally attributed to Moses — a fugitive shepherd later called to confront Pharaoh and lead his people to freedom. Old Testament, historical/law genre.
tells the story of creation, humanity made in God's image, the fall into sin, and God's unfolding relationship with Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph. main themes: creation, human identity as God's image-bearer, sin and God's mercy, and the beginnings of God's covenant with His people.
