↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

the first book of the Old Testament, traditionally attributed to Moses. its name means "origin" or "beginning."
it opens with the creation of the world, then moves through the fall of humanity, the flood, and the early history of Israel's patriarchs — Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph.
its main themes are creation, covenant, and God's promise to build a people for Himself.
