Jude | what is it
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

a short New Testament epistle traditionally attributed to Jude, brother of James and likely half-brother of Jesus.
written to urge believers to contend for the faith against false teachers who had crept into the church.
its main themes are discernment, perseverance, and God's power to keep believers from falling — closing with one of the New Testament's best-known doxologies.
