traditionally attributed to Solomon, part of the Old Testament wisdom books. a search for meaning in life "under the sun" — work, pleasure, wealth, time — that keeps circling back to the same conclusion: apart from God, it's all fleeting. ends by pointing beyond the search, to fearing God and keeping His commands.
