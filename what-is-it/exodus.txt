the second book of the bible, written by Moses — a fugitive shepherd called back to confront Pharaoh and lead his people to freedom. Old Testament, historical/law genre.
tells the story of Israel's slavery in Egypt, Moses's calling, the ten plagues, the exodus itself, and the giving of the law at Mount Sinai. main themes: deliverance, God's covenant faithfulness, and God revealing His own name and character to His people.
