the fourth book of the Old Testament, traditionally attributed to Moses. it takes its name from the two censuses of the israelites recorded in it, and covers their journey through the wilderness from Mount Sinai to the edge of the promised land. it mixes narrative (grumbling, rebellion, testing) with law and instruction, including the priestly blessing in chapter 6.
