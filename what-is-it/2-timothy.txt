a letter from the apostle Paul to Timothy, his young protégé, written from prison near the end of Paul's life — his last known letter. New Testament, part of the pastoral epistles. it's a farewell charge: stay faithful, guard the truth you were taught, don't be ashamed of the gospel even under pressure. its most quoted line — Scripture is God-breathed, useful for teaching and correction — sits inside that larger appeal to keep standing on what's true when everything else is shifting.
