a Gospel written by Luke, a physician and companion of Paul who wasn't an eyewitness himself but carefully gathered eyewitness accounts, addressed to a Gentile reader. a careful, orderly account of Jesus' life, with particular attention to the poor, the outcast, and the overlooked.
