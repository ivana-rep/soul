a letter from the apostle Paul to churches in the region of Galatia, written to defend the gospel of grace against teachers insisting Gentile believers had to follow Jewish law to be saved. New Testament, epistle. central themes: freedom in Christ, salvation by faith rather than works of the law, and living guided by the Holy Spirit rather than the sinful nature.
