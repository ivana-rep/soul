a letter from the apostle Paul to Titus, another of his trusted protégés, left to organize and lead the church on the island of Crete. New Testament, part of the pastoral epistles — practical instructions on church leadership, sound teaching, and how grace should shape everyday conduct, paired with a clear-eyed warning about handling people who cause division.
