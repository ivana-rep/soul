1 Corinthians | what is it
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

a New Testament epistle written by the apostle Paul to the church in Corinth.
addresses divisions within the church, moral failures, and confusion over spiritual gifts and worship.
its main themes are unity, holiness, love — famously described in chapter 13 — and the hope of the resurrection.
