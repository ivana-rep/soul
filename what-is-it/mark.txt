the shortest and likely earliest of the four gospels, traditionally attributed to John Mark — not one of the twelve apostles himself, but a companion of Peter and Paul believed to have written down Peter's own eyewitness account. fast-paced, action-driven — Jesus in motion, teaching and healing with urgency. widely believed to be a source both Matthew and Luke drew from.
