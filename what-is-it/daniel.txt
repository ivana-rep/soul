an Old Testament book set during Israel's exile in Babylon, following Daniel — a young Israelite deported to serve in the Babylonian court, known for interpreting dreams and surviving the lions' den for refusing to stop praying. mixes court narrative with apocalyptic visions of empires rising and falling, and of a coming kingdom that will never end.
