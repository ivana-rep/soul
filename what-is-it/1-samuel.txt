the ninth book of the bible, part of a larger historical account traditionally grouped with 2 Samuel and 1-2 Kings, covering Israel's transition from judges to monarchy. Old Testament, historical narrative.
follows Samuel — the last of Israel's judges and a prophet — as he anoints Saul as Israel's first king, then later anoints David, a shepherd boy, to succeed him. main themes: God's sovereignty over leadership, the cost of Israel's demand for a king like other nations, and the contrast between outward appearance and the heart God actually looks at.
