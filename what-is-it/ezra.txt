a historical book recounting the return of the Jewish exiles from Babylon to Jerusalem and the rebuilding of the temple, led in its second half by Ezra, a priest and scribe who brings the law of Moses back to the center of the people's life — old testament, closely tied to Nehemiah, focused on restoration, worship and faithfulness to the covenant.
