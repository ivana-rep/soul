\ i too bless you, poor as i am, but !!always cheerful and content!! with what God disposes.

---

"true poverty is always joyful," writes Anthony of Padua, echoing Francis of Assisi's "poverty with gladness."
it couldn't be otherwise: poverty, understood and embraced spiritually — which naturally carries a concrete dimension of sobriety and sharing — is recognizing that God alone is our wealth, and every good thing comes from Him.
the other kind is called misery, and it cries out for justice before God.
