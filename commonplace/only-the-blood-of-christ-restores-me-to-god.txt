only the blood of Christ restores me to God

\ the cross of Jesus is where i can lay down my shame.
\ it's where i release the lies that say i must punish myself for the hurt i've caused others.
\ it's where i trade my self-hatred for His mercy.
\ if there's one reason i cling to my faith more than anything else, it's because i know how much i need Jesus.
\ i need healing and forgiveness.
\ i need someone to do for me what i cannot do for myself.
\ !!everywhere we look today, it seems the world is offering cheap substitutes, ways to numb guilt, ways to redefine love, ways to try to earn forgiveness, but nothing comes close to what Jesus has done on the cross!!.
\ only the blood of Christ restores me to God, and only His resurrection makes me fully alive again.
\ that's the power of His sacrificial love for us that demands our worship.
\ and not the kind of worship that makes us pretend we've got it all together.
\ but worship that brings our sincerity—all of ourselves, including the brokenness—before the One who already carried our brokenness for us.
\ our everyday hallelujah is the gratitude pouring out of our hearts because our debt has been paid.
