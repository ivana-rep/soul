\ every day you have the choice to:
\ * complain or give thanks
\ * blame others or examine yourself
\ * feed your fear or strengthen your faith
\ * speak poorly of others or bless them
\ * give up or remain steadfast
\ * assume the worst or think on what is true
\ * hold onto bitterness or choose forgiveness
\ * react in the flesh or walk by the Spirit
\ * seek people's approval or seek God's
\ * lean on your own understanding or trust the Lord
\ * live like the world or live by God's Word
\ 
\ !!following Jesus isn't one big decision.
\ it's a thousand little ones, every day!!.
