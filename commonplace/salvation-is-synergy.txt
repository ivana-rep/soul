\ salvation is not something God does to you — it's something God does with you. His grace does not override your will, it waits for your yes and works through it. think of Peter stepping out of the boat: it's not his faith alone or Christ's power alone that keeps him from sinking — !!it's his hand reaching and Christ's hand grasping it!!. every small act of faithfulness is you cooperating with that grace, never decoration on top of it, because a will healed by God is still your will, never erased into a puppet's.
