\ the cure to a lonely heart is to be alone with God.
\ because loneliness
\ isn't the absence of people,
\ it's the absence of connection to the One who made you.
\ sit with Him.
\ speak to Him.
\ !!let His presence fill the quiet!!.
\ you'll realize, you were never truly alone,
\ just waiting to feel His nearness again.
