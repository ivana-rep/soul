\ [Solomon], the richest, most handsome, most popular man in the bible — the guy who had sex with more than 1,000 women — had it all. he ended up depressed at the end of his life and wrote a book about how everything is pointless.
\ that sex, that fame, that money, that status you're pursuing is never going to satisfy you.
\ there's eternity written in every human heart — !!a God shaped hole in your heart that only God can fill!!. you keep trying to fill it with worldly success, clout, followers, money.
\ that's the devil's goal: get you to pursue all of that so you never realize the only thing that can satisfy your soul is God Himself — a true, genuine relationship with Jesus.
