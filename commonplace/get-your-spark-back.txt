\ 25 uncomfortable ways to get your spark back for God.
\ 1.  get up early to pray, even when you don't want to.
\ 2.  fast from food, or from whatever distracts you.
\ 3.  cut off the thing you already know is pulling you away.
\ 4.  put the phone down and sit in silence.
\ 5.  read Scripture even when it feels dry.
\ 6.  forgive someone who hasn't earned it.
\ 7.  own where you've been wrong.
\ 8.  bring the habit you're hiding into the light.
\ 9.  serve without needing anyone to notice.
\ 10. give money precisely when it's uncomfortable.
\ 11. sit with conviction instead of running from it.
\ 12. choose solitude over constant noise.
\ 13. release control and trust God with the outcome.
\ 14. stop reaching for whatever numbs you.
\ 15. tell the truth, even when it costs you something.
\ 16. show up consistently, not just when you feel like it.
\ 17. put distance between yourself and what's pulling you down.
\ 18. pick discipline over motivation.
\ 19. pray past the point where you want to stop.
\ 20. worship when you feel nothing.
\ 21. stay patient through the silent seasons.
\ 22. trade a bad habit for a godly one.
\ 23. seek correction instead of approval.
\ 24. love the people who've hurt you.
\ 25. hand your plans over to God's timing.
