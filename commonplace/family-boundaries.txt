family boundaries

↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

\ we've confused loyalty with true love.
\
\ family is often used to demand tolerance:
\ "be the bigger person."
\ "honor them."
\ "don't break the family."
\
\ so harm gets minimized.
\ truth gets silenced.
\ and dysfunction gets baptized as loyalty.
\
\ family is a gift.
\ family is not God.
\
\ God designed family.
\ idolatry distorts it.
\
\ not because family is bad.
\ but because idolatry is subtle.
\ when family comes first, Jesus gets pushed to second.
\
\ primary allegiance belongs to Jesus.
\ discipleship has a cost.
\
\ "anyone who loves their father or mother more than me is not worthy of me."
\ —[Matthew 10:37](post.html?p=bible/matthew_10-37.txt)
\
\ what we tolerate, we enable and normalize.
\
\ we've been taught family loyalty means silence.
\ love means unlimited access.
\ forgiveness means staying.
\
\ if you confront it, you're "unloving."
\ if you leave, you're "rebelling."
\
\ the Bible never tells you to tolerate sin because it shares your last name.
\
\ love confronts.
\ wisdom limits access.
\ holiness draws lines.
\
\ "have nothing to do with the fruitless deeds of darkness, but rather expose them."
\ —[Ephesians 5:11](post.html?p=bible/ephesians_5-11.txt)
\
\ forgiveness does not mean access.
\ grace does not mean proximity.
\ mercy does not require self-betrayal.
\
\ !!if your faith requires you to stay silent while sin destroys you, you were taught the wrong version of Christianity.!!
