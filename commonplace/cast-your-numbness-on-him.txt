\ there's a kind of quiet that isn't peace. it's just numbness — and it shows up right when shame starts talking, replaying the same regrets like they're still up for a vote on who you are.
\
\ they aren't. what happened already happened. it doesn't get a say in what comes next unless you hand it one.
\
\ !!lay the shame down. let God hand you a heart that isn't scarred anymore.!!
\
\ you'll still stumble. that was never the condition — the condition was never carrying it alone.
