\ in early 1941, a few weeks before the Gestapo arrested Him, [Maximilian Kolbe](post.html?p=saints/maximilian-kolbe.txt) wrote to a friar stranded in Japan by the war:
\ "avoid sadness and worries, !!because there's no reason to be sad!!. is the world not guided by divine providence?"
\ a few months later He died in Auschwitz, in place of a man He didn't know.
