\ when we belong to Jesus, the Holy Spirit lives in us and begins changing us from the inside out.
\ the "fruit" is the character He grows in us as He makes us more like Jesus:
\
\ 1. love — want what is good for others.
\ 2. joy — find gladness in God, even on hard days.
\ 3. peace — rest in God, even when you feel afraid.
\ 4. patience — wait without becoming angry.
\ 5. kindness — treat others with care.
\ 6. goodness — choose what is right.
\ 7. faithfulness — keep trusting God and keep your word.
\ 8. gentleness — use your strength without being harsh.
\ 9. self-control — say no to wrong desires.
\
\ !!this fruit does not make us belong to Jesus.
\ it grows because we belong to Jesus.!!
