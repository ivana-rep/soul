\ being gentle means !!being a brother to all!!;
\ being humble means being a true Friar Minor.

---

being gentle is sometimes mistaken for being weak, submissive — someone who minds their own business and doesn't get involved in anyone else's.
but from a Franciscan point of view, the whole thing flips: the gentle one is exactly the one who truly knows how to build deeply fraternal relationships with everyone, precisely because he doesn't want to prevail and doesn't know the language of violence.
others matter to him, because they are a gift from God!
