\ why did Jesus have to die for me to be forgiven?
\ why couldn't he just forgive me from Heaven?
\ easier explained with a story.
\ 
\ once upon a time, in a kingdom, there was a good king who was known to be both just and loving.
\ and this king, he noticed one day that somebody kept stealing from his treasury.
\ and he wrote a command saying whoever this person is that gets caught stealing, they have to serve 10 lashes.
\ now, month after month, the theft didn't stop and he was angry.
\ he said, well, somebody keeps stealing. he wrote a new command saying that this person, when they get caught, they're gonna bear 30 lashes.
\ the theft never stopped. and finally he said, alright, i've had enough. whoever this person is, is going to serve 40 lashes.
\ but the problem with 40 lashes is that that is an automatic death sentence.
\ one day, the person got caught, and the person who was stealing from his treasury was his very own daughter.
\ 
\ now, since the king is a just king, he said, this command needs to stand.
\ the person needs to be punished.
\ i can't change my rule, because if i did, i wouldn't be a just king.
\ and so one day, when the time came for his daughter to receive these lashes, as she's walking to the podium,
\ she takes off her shirt, and the person's about to start whipping her, and they hear a scream saying, stop!
\ 
\ it was the king. the king comes up to the podium, he said, you know what?
\ i'm gonna take this punishment instead.
\ and he takes off his shirt, he lays across his daughter, and he said to the executioner, "go ahead, start the whipping."
\ and the executioner said, are you sure, king? you're going to die.
\ and he said, "the ruling must stand." and so the whipping started, and the king died instead of his daughter.
\ 
\ now, some people said, the king shouldn't have done that.
\ if he hadn't done that, he wouldn't be loving.
\ others say, well, the king should have just cancelled that execution.
\ but if that was the case, then the king wouldn't be a just king.
\ he would break his own rules.
\ 
\ !!Jesus had to die for us because the wages and the punishment of sin is death!!.
\ somebody had to pay for your sin.
\ if you're standing in front of a judge for doing something illegal, the judge can't say, wow, you look like you're sorry. you can go now.
\ no, because then the judge would not be a just judge.
\ somebody needs to pay for sin.
\ and Jesus did that for you.
\ He is the justifier of the ungodly.
\ He took His own life and put it over your life.
\ and got beat. the same way that king got beat to death for his daughter,
\ that is why Jesus had to die for your sin.
\ because He's both loving and just.
