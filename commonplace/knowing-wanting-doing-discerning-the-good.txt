\ !!the first grace, knowing how to do good; the second, wanting to do good; the third, doing good; the fourth, discerning the good.!!

---

know, want, do, discern: this, perhaps in a different order, could be a good indication of a path and a method for setting out briskly on the gospel road of doing good.
if nothing else because it reminds us that "doing" is still nothing, or at least fragile and short-lived, if it isn't accompanied by passion and will, but also by perseverance.
but before anything else, by discernment—that is, by thought.
