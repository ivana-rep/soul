\ you guys think this has been easy for me?
\ i come back to Jesus,
\ i'm sacrificing things i never thought i would lose.
\ i'm judged, i'm talked about.
\ i'm isolated, i'm alone.
\ i'm corrected by the Lord,
\ i'm humbled.
\ i go through trials, tribulations,
\ i'm discouraged, and yet i have to pick myself back up.
\
\ i'm not putting on a show for you all to look religious and to look holy.
\ i don't need to do that.
\ i have a mug shot floating around out there somewhere.
\ don't look it up, okay?
\
\ but the reason that i'm still here,
\ that i'm still standing, is because !!i believe in the depths of my soul
\ that Jesus Christ rose from the dead on the third day.!!
\ all the rest of the stuff is just collateral.
\
\ we've already talked about that in the course of church history over the past 2,000 years.
\ we've already talked about it in a basic theology 101 course.
\
\ i'm not asking you, can humans be morally correct without religion?
\ no, i'm asking you, did Jesus walk out of that grave?
\ cause that's what christianity hinges upon.
\ cause if He didn't, then it all falls apart.
\ if you truly believe that He didn't,
\ then i have some questions for you.
\
\ what about Peter, who made a resolve in his heart to walk away, to be a fisherman? what changed?
\ what about Paul, who was persecuting christians?
\ he had an encounter with Jesus, and all of a sudden,
\ now he's one of the most well known people in the faith.
\ what changed? they saw Jesus.
\ what about all of the prophecies in the Old Testament that led up that were pointing to this one person, Jesus?
\ the overwhelming evidence points to the fact that He walked out of that grave.
\ and so you can too.
