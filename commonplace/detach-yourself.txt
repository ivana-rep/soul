\ God will not let you miss what's meant for you if you are intentionally pursuing Christ.
\ !!you can detach yourself from anything!! and everything you're gripping onto —
\ because when you let it go, if it's meant to be in your life, it will swing right on back.
\ you are not meant to carry the full weight of something on your shoulders.
\ God is carrying that with you.
\ it's meant for you. God will make it happen. but you don't need to carry it alone.
