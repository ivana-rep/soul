jesus never said "give them another chance"
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=commonplace/gesu-non-ha-mai-detto-dategli-unaltra-possibilita.txt)

\ we've confused grace with giving more chances.
\
\ they harm you.
\ you forgive them.
\ they do it again.
\
\ so you give them another chance.
\ and another.
\ and another.
\
\ because christians are supposed to show "grace."
\ but grace is a gift, not an entitlement.
\
\ grace isn't gullibility.
\
\ grace is undeserved favor.
\ it can mean giving someone another opportunity.
\ but scripture never defines grace as automatically giving someone a second chance.
\
\ patterns matter.
\ repentance matters.
\ fruit matters.
\ grace can be generous without being gullible.
\
\ grace doesn't guarantee a second chance.
\
\ Adam and Eve received God's mercy and grace.
\ but they still were removed from the garden — they didn't get to go back.
\
\ Moses was forgiven.
\ but he still didn't get to enter the Promised Land.
\
\ grace can forgive the failure without giving another chance.
\
\ as christians, we're called to be gracious people.
\ but grace doesn't mean giving everyone a second chance.
\
\ you can show grace and still...
\ end a relationship after infidelity.
\ leave after a single act of abuse.
\ distance after serious harm.
\
\ grace can be generous without giving someone another opportunity to significantly harm you.
\
\ grace calls for change, not continued chaos.
\
\ "should we keep on sinning so that God can show us more and more of his wonderful grace? of course not!"
\ — [Romans 6:1-2](post.html?p=bible/romans_6-1-2.txt)
\
\ biblical grace doesn't say: "keep harming me. i'll keep allowing it."
\ grace isn't permission to persist in a pattern.
\
\ true repentance produces change.
\
\ Jesus said, "prove by the way you live that you have repented of your sins and turned to God."
\ — [Matthew 3:8](post.html?p=bible/matthew_3-8.txt)
\
\ an apology can happen in a moment.
\ repentance produces fruit over time.
\
\ don't just listen for "i'm sorry."
\ look for a shift in the pattern.
\
\ at times, Paul encouraged limited chances too.
\
\ "if people are causing divisions among you, give a first and second warning. after that, have nothing more to do with them."
\ — [Titus 3:10](post.html?p=bible/titus_3-10.txt)
\
\ Paul didn't call endless chances grace.
\ sometimes grace gives a second chance.
\ sometimes grace gives multiple chances.
\ but sometimes in wisdom, grace simply walks away.
\
\ grace can't be demanded.
\
\ you can...
\
\ forgive without forgetting the harm.
\ love without leaving yourself vulnerable.
\ show mercy without maintaining proximity.
\ !!give grace without giving another chance.!!
