\ what i love about atheists
\ is they've made up their mind about what they believe and they live it.
\ and then we as christians, being the ones that claim to love Jesus,
\ are willingly and repeatedly partaking in the very things that nailed Him to that cross.
\
\ if that's the case, and i'm asking this genuinely,
\ if that's the life we're gonna choose,
\ not talking about people that struggle,
\ not talking about people that fall,
\ i'm talking about people that have chosen this.
\
\ what was the point of His suffering?
\ what was the point of the lashes on His back and the pierced feet?
\ so that we could go to the club and black out while wearing a cross necklace.
\
\ for those of you,
\ for those of you that are gonna say i'm judging cause i just said that that story is about me.
\ i don't think we know why Jesus went to the cross.
\ i need you to know this. He laid down everything for you.
\
\ because whether you realize it or acknowledge it or not, the Lord calls certain things sin for a reason.
\ it's not arbitrary, it's not random.
\ it's because these things are in direct opposition to how He designed life to function.
\ it's harmful to you and the people around you.
\
\ Romans says the result of sin is you're not gonna find life,
\ you're not gonna find joy,
\ you're not gonna find peace there.
\ that's what God has been trying to warn us and tell us about this whole time.
\ that what you think is freedom is really just slavery.
\ what you think is pleasure is really just poison disguised.
\ and i know because i drank it.
\
\ but the good news is that what the Father did through Jesus on the cross
\ is the antidote to the poison that you've drink.
\ is the remedy to the wounds you've sustained because of it.
\
\ and i promise you, i promise you,
\ i promise you, i promise you,
\ i promise you, i promise you.
\ that what you will find in Jesus,
\ the promises that He has promised.
\ that the love, the joy, the peace, the freedom.
\ is so much better than anything sin has ever promised you.
\ because all of that was just lies, right?
\ cause !!sin never really delivers.
\ but i know a deliverer.!!
