\ for a christian, getting sick and going to the doctor isn't a lapse of faith.
\ !!prayer and medicine have never been rivals!!: someone can pray the rosary in the chapel
\ while the surgeon operates down the hall, and both are asking for the same thing.
\
\ !!faith was never a promise of a body that doesn't break!!.
\ Paul carried a [thorn in his flesh](post.html?p=bible/2-corinthians_12-7-10.txt#v7) and begged three times for it to be taken away;
\ the answer he got was "My grace is all you need" ([2 Corinthians 12:9](post.html?p=bible/2-corinthians_12-7-10.txt#v9)), not a cure.
\ the apostles got sick, got hurt, got old like everyone else.
\ a miracle is an exceptional gift, not the ordinary way God expects us to get better,
\ and refusing treatment while waiting for one would be putting Him to the test.
\ Jesus Himself said that sick people need a doctor ([Matthew 9:12](post.html?p=bible/matthew_9-9-13.txt#v12)),
\ and Luke, who wrote a gospel, was one (Colossians 4:14).
\
\ !!caring for the sick is one of the first things christians were known for!!.
\ in the fourth century the [emperor Julian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_%28emperor%29#Charity), who wanted to bring back the old gods,
\ complained in a letter that the "Galileans" looked after not only their own poor but the pagans' too,
\ while the temples did nothing.
\ in a world where the weak and the destitute were usually left to themselves,
\ bishops and monasteries opened [xenodochia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenodochium), free houses for strangers, the poor and the sick.
\ around 370 [Basil of Caesarea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basil_of_Caesarea#Caesarea) built a whole complex of them outside his city, with physicians and nurses:
\ the [Basiliad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basiliad) is often counted as the first hospital in the modern sense.
\ the reason was the gospel: "I was sick, and you cared for me" ([Matthew 25:36](post.html?p=bible/matthew_25-31-46.txt#v36)).
\
\ the same goes for science. !!the [war between faith and reason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict_thesis) is mostly a story told from one side!!.
\ the [first universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_university) grew out of [cathedral schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_school),
\ [Gregor Mendel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregor_Mendel), the father of genetics, was an Augustinian friar,
\ and the [big bang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Bang) theory was first put forward by [Georges Lemaître](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Lema%C3%AEtre), a Catholic priest.
\ they studied creation because they believed it was made with an order that could be understood.
\
\ !!a christian can pray and get treated, trust God and trust the doctor, with no contradiction:!!
\ !!the hands that heal are one of the ways He answers.!!
