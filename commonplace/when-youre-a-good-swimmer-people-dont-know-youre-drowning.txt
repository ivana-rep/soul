\ when you're a good swimmer, people don't know you're drowning.

---

this quote captures the reality of functional suffering, the idea that strong, competent people often hide their struggles behind a mask of self-sufficiency, causing others to miss their pain.
the exact quote is modern, several bible verses directly address hidden pain, the facade of strength, God's awareness of unspoken struggles, and the fact that we should give up on self-sufficiency, to abide in God.
