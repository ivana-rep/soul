\ gospel means "good news", and the news is this.
\ God made us for Himself.
\ sin pulled us away from Him, and nothing we do on our own can close that distance.
\ so Jesus came: He lived the perfect life we can't live, He took the death we had earned, and He rose again.
\ whoever turns back and puts their trust in Him is forgiven, counted righteous, and given eternal life.
\ 
\ the gospel is not "be good, and God will love you".
\ it's !!"Jesus was good in your place, because God already loves you"!!.
