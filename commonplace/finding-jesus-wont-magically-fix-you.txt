\ !!finding Jesus didn't fix me.!!
\ i still lose my temper.
\ i still struggle with temptation, and i still judge people.
\ i still get jealous, insecure, and selfish.
\ and there are days where i know exactly what He wants me to do, and i do the opposite.
\
\ and for a long time, that confused me.
\ i thought the closer you got to God, the more put together you'd feel.
\ but that's not what happened.
\ what happened was the opposite.
\ !!the more i saw Him, the more clearly i could see everything in me that still wasn't right.
\ and i think that's exactly what's supposed to happen.
\ you don't notice how dirty your room is until someone turns the lights on.!!
\ the light doesn't make it messy.
\ it just shows you what was always there.
\
\ [1 John 1:8](post.html?p=bible/1-john_1-8.txt). if we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.
\
\ the walk isn't about performing goodness for other people.
\ it's about being honest enough with yourself and with God to admit when you fall short.
\
\ !!Jesus didn't make me perfect.
\ He just made it harder to pretend that i was.
\ God is an endless pursuit of us.
\ let us pursue Him also.!!
