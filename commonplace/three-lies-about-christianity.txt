\ history doesn't cancel christianity — it clarifies it.
\
\ 1. !!the Big Bang wasn't Genesis' enemy!!. it was first proposed by a [Catholic priest, Georges Lemaître](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Lema%C3%AEtre#Hypothesis_of_the_primeval_atom), and scientists at the time mocked him for sounding too much like [Genesis](post.html?p=what-is-it/genesis.txt) — a universe with a beginning, not an eternal one. Genesis was never trying to be a science manual; it answers who and why, not how.
\
\ 2. !!Galileo was never tortured!!. he stayed in papal residences. [his conflict was political, not scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galileo_affair#Trial_and_second_judgment,_1633) — [Copernicus had taught heliocentrism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copernican_heliocentrism) a century earlier without punishment. Galileo's mistake was reinterpreting scripture to win an argument, not defying it.
\
\ 3. !![the crusades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusades#Background) weren't an unprovoked invasion!!. they came four centuries after [Islamic expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Muslim_conquests) had already swallowed the historic heart of christianity, from the Holy Land to North Africa. a late, defensive response — not a clean one, but not the myth either.
