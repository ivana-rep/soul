\ history doesn't cancel christianity — it clarifies it.
\
\ 1. !!the Big Bang wasn't Genesis' enemy!!. it was first proposed by a Catholic priest, Georges Lemaître, and scientists at the time mocked him for sounding too much like Genesis — a universe with a beginning, not an eternal one. Genesis was never trying to be a science manual; it answers who and why, not how.
\
\ 2. !!Galileo was never tortured!!. he stayed in papal residences. his conflict was political, not scientific — Copernicus had taught heliocentrism a century earlier without punishment. Galileo's mistake was reinterpreting scripture to win an argument, not defying it.
\
\ 3. !!the crusades weren't an unprovoked invasion!!. they came four centuries after Islamic expansion had already swallowed the historic heart of christianity, from the Holy Land to North Africa. a late, defensive response — not a clean one, but not the myth either.
