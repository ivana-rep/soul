forgiveness isn't reconciliation

↳ [back to archive](archive.html)

\ !!forgiveness and reconciliation are not the same thing.!!
\
\ forgiveness means letting go of resentment — it doesn't require going back to the person who hurt you.
\ reconciliation takes both sides: real repentance, and trust rebuilt over time.
\ some relationships heal. some have to stay at a distance. distance isn't proof you haven't forgiven.
\
\ you're not asked to stay close to what's destroying you.
\ [Luke 6:28](post.html?p=bible/luke_6-28.txt) says bless those who curse you, pray for those who hurt you — not move back in with them.
\ you can pray for someone's repentance without handing them access to keep hurting you.
\
\ you're not asked to trust everyone either.
\ [Matthew 10:16](post.html?p=bible/matthew_10-16.txt) says be shrewd as snakes, harmless as doves. love without discernment isn't grace, it's naivety.
\ !!boundaries and grace aren't opposites.!!
\
\ and you're not asked to pretend nothing happened.
\ [Matthew 18:15](post.html?p=bible/matthew_18-15.txt) says go to them privately, not bury it in silence — truth still comes before peace.
\
\ !!mercy and wisdom were never meant to cancel each other out.!!
