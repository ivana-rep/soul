forgiveness isn't reconciliation
↳ [back to index](index.html)
↳ [back to archive](archive.html)
↳ en // [it](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul-it/post.html?p=commonplace/il-perdono-non-e-riconciliazione.txt)

\ !!forgiveness releases the debt. it doesn't hand over everything else.!!
\ it means giving up your own claim to be paid back, and leaving justice to God ([Romans 12:19](post.html?p=bible/romans_12-19-21.txt)).
\ it doesn't mean pretending nothing happened, cancelling the consequences, or switching off your judgment.
\
\ it isn't healing. forgiving is a decision, healing is a process:
\ you can forgive today and still be hurting tomorrow.
\
\ it isn't reconciliation. forgiving takes one, reconciling takes two:
\ real repentance, and trust rebuilt over time. some relationships heal, some have to stay at a distance —
\ and distance isn't proof you haven't forgiven.
\ [Luke 6:28](post.html?p=bible/luke_6-17-49.txt#v28) says bless those who curse you, pray for those who hurt you — not move back in with them.
\
\ it isn't trust. forgiving isn't forgetting.
\ you can forgive someone and still see that they lie or manipulate — you know a tree by its fruit ([Matthew 7:16](post.html?p=bible/matthew_5-1-7-29.txt)).
\ [Matthew 10:16](post.html?p=bible/matthew_10-16.txt) says be shrewd as snakes, harmless as doves. love without discernment isn't grace, it's naivety.
\ !!boundaries and grace aren't opposites.!!
\
\ it isn't the end of consequences. you can forgive and still report, hold limits, protect whoever needs protecting.
\ Paul left Alexander to God, and still told Timothy to watch out for him ([2 Timothy 4:14-15](post.html?p=bible/2-timothy_4-14-15.txt)).
\ the prudent see danger coming and take cover ([Proverbs 22:3](post.html?p=bible/proverbs_22-3.txt)).
\
\ it isn't going back to how things were. even after reconciliation, trust is rebuilt slowly,
\ and some doors stay different from before.
\
\ and it isn't silence. [Matthew 18:15](post.html?p=bible/matthew_18-15.txt) says go to them privately, not bury it — truth still comes before peace.
\
\ turning the other cheek ([Matthew 5:39](post.html?p=bible/matthew_5-1-7-29.txt#v39)) doesn't contradict any of this:
\ it forbids paying back the blow, not drawing a line.
\ when Jesus Himself was struck, He didn't hit back — He asked why ([John 18:22-23](post.html?p=bible/john_18-22-23.txt)).
\ but the same passage asks for more than not retaliating: give the cloak too, love your enemy, pray for them.
\ no revenge, still wanting their good, and the wisdom to stay safe — all at once.
\
\ !!mercy and wisdom were never meant to cancel each other out.!!
