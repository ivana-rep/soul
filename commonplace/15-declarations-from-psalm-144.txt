\ fifteen things Psalm 144 says about who God is for me:
\
\ 1.  God is my rock — when everything else moves, He stays put. I build on Him. (144:1)
\ 2.  God trains my hands for war — no fight is wasted, each one is a lesson from Him. (144:1)
\ 3.  God sharpens my skill in every battle — even the small, unseen tasks are shaping me for something. (144:1)
\ 4.  God is my loving ally — He doesn't put up with me, He loves me through and through. (144:2)
\ 5.  God is my fortress — my safety isn't in my own strength, it's in His. (144:2)
\ 6.  God is my tower of safety — when trouble comes, there's somewhere to run, and it holds. (144:2)
\ 7.  God is my rescuer — He doesn't just shield me from a distance, He steps in and pulls me out. (144:2)
\ 8.  God is my shield — whatever is thrown at me lands on Him first. (144:2)
\ 9.  I take refuge in God — !!He is not where I end up when everything else fails, He is where I go first!!. (144:2)
\ 10. God subdues what rises against me — what was meant to bring me down ends up under my feet. He fights for me. (144:2)
\ 11. God takes notice of me — I'm not lost in the crowd: the maker of everything has me in view. (144:3)
\ 12. God thinks about me — not just watching from afar, I'm on His mind. not tolerated, loved. (144:3)
\ 13. my days are in His hands — my life is a breath, a passing shadow, and still He holds it. (144:4)
\ 14. God opens the heavens for me — He isn't far off. He comes down and moves on my behalf. (144:5)
\ 15. God touches the mountains — what looks immovable to me is nothing to Him. (144:5)
