start with the Gospels, New Testament:
1. [John](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1&version=NLT) — God of the world, the Son of God
2. [Mark](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+1&version=NLT) — God of the Romans, the suffering servant
3. [Matthew](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+1&version=NLT) — God of the Jews, the Kingdom of God
4. [Luke](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+1&version=NLT) — God of the outcast, the perfect man

these four books teach who Jesus is — his life, his works, his presence on earth.

then move to the Old Testament:
1. [Genesis](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+1&version=NLT) — God of promise, beginnings
2. [Exodus](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+1&version=NLT) — God of power, deliverance
3. [Leviticus](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+1&version=NLT) — God of purity, instruction
4. [Numbers](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Numbers+1&version=NLT) — God of perseverance, journeys
5. [Deuteronomy](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+1&version=NLT) — God of preparation, obedience

these five books teach the fall of man, God's chosen people, and the Law.

back to the New Testament:
1. [Romans](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+1&version=NLT) — God of righteousness
2. [Galatians](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+1&version=NLT) — God of justification, freedom in Christ
3. [Colossians](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Colossians+1&version=NLT) — God of preeminence, supremacy of Christ

these three books extend the teaching on the Law, Christ's sacrifice, and what it means to walk in the spirit.

then wisdom:
1. [Proverbs](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+1&version=NLT) — God of prudence, practical wisdom
2. [Ecclesiastes](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ecclesiastes+1&version=NLT) — God of purpose, the meaning of life

and finally, suffering:
1. [Job](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job+1&version=NLT) — God of pain, blessings through suffering
2. [1 Peter](https://beta.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Peter+1&version=NLT) — God of persecution, responding to suffering

these last two remind us that hardship reaches Christians too — and to seek God for Himself, not for our own comfort or righteousness.
