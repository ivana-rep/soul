read the bible as if you were a non-believer. it's the best way to read it deeply — questioning it fully, so you can actually reckon with the Word instead of skimming past it.

six questions, worked through with scripture alongside. not proof, a path.

↳ source: the bible of the non-believer, fernando woes iga

7 for He is our God.
we are the people He watches over, the flock under His care.
if only you would listen to His voice today!

13 if you look for Me wholeheartedly,
you will find Me.

~ Psalms 95:7 // Jeremiah 29:13

↳ #1 — why need God?
we've all sinned, every one of us. so we need something, or someone, outside ourselves to redeem us from this fallen nature. that's the starting point — not guilt for its own sake, an honest diagnosis.
Romans 3:23 | we all fall short"we all fall short of God's glorious standard"
↳ #2 — God's gift
if we're all sinners, being a good person isn't enough to earn Heaven on our own. that's exactly where God's gift comes in: eternal life through Jesus, even as sinners. not earned. given.
Romans 6:23 | the free gift of God"the free gift of God is eternal life"
Romans 5:8 | while we were still sinners"while we were still sinners"
↳ #3 — God loves us, as sinners
He doesn't wait for us to clean up first. He loves us as we are — sinners — and gave Himself for us anyway. that's not a small thing to sit with.
John 3:16 | God so loved the world"will not perish but have eternal life"
↳ #4 — our response
if all of this is true, the only honest response is to actually follow Him, not just admire Him from a distance. we cannot bear good fruit if we're not near the vine — apart from Him we can do nothing.
John 14:6 | I am the way"I am the way, the truth, and the life"
John 14:8-15 | seen the Father"anyone who has seen Me has seen the Father"
John 14:21-27 | peace as a gift"I am leaving you with a gift—peace of mind and heart"
John 15:1-13 | remain in Me"you cannot be fruitful unless you remain in Me"
↳ #5 — Jesus is God
did Jesus actually claim to be God? He did. God revealed His own name to Moses as "I AM" — and Jesus took that same name for Himself. if He was only a teacher, why bother obeying Him? but if He is God, everything changes: the teaching, the preaching, the peace He offered — all of it makes sense because He is the Lord.
John 8:51-52 | will never die"My teaching will never die"
John 8:56-59 | before Abraham was, I AM"I AM"
Exodus 3:14 | I AM who I AM"I AM WHO I AM"
John 11:25-30 | I am the resurrection and the life"I am the resurrection and the life"
↳ #6 — He is good
even setting faith aside for a moment: read the Sermon on the Mount. look at what He actually taught. He knew what's good for us — the ethics hold up on their own, even for someone who isn't ready to believe yet.
Matthew 5:1-48 | the Sermon on the Mount"you are to be perfect, even as your Father in Heaven is perfect"
